Zone 2 ● Company tree

North Star Metric

Primary compass, with explicit safeguards

Primary metric

Total Activities Logged

The direct expression of core utility. An upload means the user trained, synced telemetry, and re-entered the loop. It drives feed density, retention, and paywall exposures.

Why not MRR?

Lagging business output. Targeting it directly pushes aggressive paywalls that erode the free network — the thing that fills the funnel.

Constellation safeguards

Risk mitigation

1. Goodhart's Law
Risk: users gaming streaks with empty 1-min manual logs. Counter: GPS-Verified Activity Rate & Median Session Duration.
2. Seasonality
Risk: winter dips read as failure. Counter: 12-week rolling average and YoY cohort curves.
3. Power-user skew
Risk: top 5% hides churn in the breadth of the base. Counter: pair NSM with Weekly Active Loggers (WAL).
Pair the NSM with WAL so we measure both depth and breadth. Treating this as a constellation, not a single number, is the defence against gaming and seasonal noise.

Zone 3 ● Activation squad

Squad Mandate

What breaks if we disappear, and how we measure success

Mandate

Solve the cold start

If we disappear, the top of the funnel becomes a leaking bucket. New users download, see an empty feed, fail to pair sensors, and churn.

Acquisition spend is wasted before NSM ever sees the user.

Scope: first 7 days. Setup, first activity, first social tie.
Main squad metric

Activation Rate

% of new users who log ≥ 1 activity AND follow ≥ 1 friend in their first 7 days.
Why dual-criteria?

Strava lives at the intersection of tracking and community. A user who logs alone (no kudos) churns ~50% faster. A user who only follows but never logs is a passive lurker. Real activation needs both.

The dual-criteria definition is deliberate. Defend it: optimising for first-run alone produces ghost users; optimising for follows alone produces lurkers.

Zone 4 ● Risks & guardrails

What Could Break

Failure modes we instrument before launch

⚠️ Goodhart Gaming
Risk: empty 30-second manual logs just to keep the streak.

Mitigation: track % of streak workouts logged via manual entry (vs GPS). Apply a 5-min minimum duration filter on manual logs.
⚠️ Burnout Push fatigue
Risk: seven days of nudges trigger a global push opt-out.

Mitigation: 14-day push opt-out rate of challenge participants vs control. Auto-throttle if delta > 2pp.
⚠️ The Cliff Post-streak drop
Risk: day-8 logging collapses once the extrinsic reward vanishes.

Mitigation: day 8–14 retention of completers vs control. Trigger a club / local-group recommendation on day 7 to convert extrinsic → intrinsic motivation.
The Cliff is the most important risk. Behavioural psych says extrinsic rewards crowd out intrinsic motivation — so we route completers into a club recommendation before the streak ends.
